# Lesson 1.1 - Generative AI Essentials

## What is Training?
- Training = learning from past mistakes
- Example: Lemonade stand - Day 1 wrong, Day 100 right

## Discriminative vs Generative
- Discriminative (Detective Dan) = judges only. Example: "Is this a cat or dog?"
- Generative (Artist Amy) = creates new things. Example: "Draw a cat with wings"

## Neural Networks
- Factory workers in a line
- Weights = trust between workers

## GANs
- Generator = forger (creates fakes)
- Discriminator = cop (spots fakes)
- They compete → both improve
- Invented 2014

## Transformers
- Looks at ALL words at once
- Attention = focuses on important words
- Example: "bank of the river" → "river" gets attention
- Invented 2017

## Foundation Models
- One model trained on everything (Leonardo da Vinci)
- Examples: GPT-4, Llama, DALL-E

## Fine-tuning
- Take foundation model, teach one specialty

## Timeline
- 2014: GANs
- 2017: Transformers
- 2022: ChatGPT
